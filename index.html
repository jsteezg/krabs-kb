Mr. Krabs — Knowledge Notes

Welcome to the knowledge-notes site for Mr. Krabs, the S-STEM Scholar Assistant for Coastline College. This site publishes the assistant's maintained notes so they can be imported, read, and kept in sync as a website knowledge source.

The notes here focus on transfer planning for Coastline College students in STEM programs, especially course articulation between Coastline and University of California campuses. Content is derived from public ASSIST articulation agreements and is written so the assistant can quote each agreement's printed cycle year and course mappings accurately.

Pages on this site

  • UCI vs UCSD Biology Comparison Note — how to compare UC Irvine and UC San Diego for Biology transfer, including the different ASSIST cycles on file (UCI 2025-26, UCSD 2026-27) and the per-school course mappings for biology, chemistry, physics, and math.
  • About This Knowledge Base — what this site is, where its content comes from, and how it is maintained.
  • Website-Source Pilot Marker — a small marker page used to verify that the assistant can import and retrieve content from this website.

How to use these notes

Students should treat these notes as a starting point and confirm every course mapping on ASSIST.org for the current academic year before finalizing a transfer plan, and review their plan with a Coastline counselor. Articulation agreements change by cycle, and each page here states the cycle its mappings come from.
