UCI vs UCSD Biology Comparison Note

READ THIS FIRST when a student asks you to COMPARE UC Irvine and UC San Diego (especially for Biology):

Two different ASSIST agreements are on file, and they are on DIFFERENT cycles. Any comparison MUST say this out loud in the answer:
- Coastline to UC Irvine: ASSIST agreement cycle 2025-26.
- Coastline to UC San Diego: ASSIST agreement cycle 2026-27.
Suggested sentence: "One important note: the ASSIST agreements on file for these two schools are on different cycles - UC Irvine is 2025-26 and UC San Diego is 2026-27 - so confirm the current-year rows on ASSIST.org before you finalize your plan."

Headline structure (from the agreements on file; always confirm row-level detail on ASSIST.org):

UC Irvine - Biological Sciences (2025-26 ASSIST):
- Biology core: Coastline BIOL C180 + C185 cover UC Irvine Bio Sci 93 and 94.
- General chemistry: Coastline CHEM C180/C180L + C185/C185L (full year with labs).
- Organic chemistry: at least the first semester is required before transfer - Coastline CHEM C220/C220L covers CHEM 51A/51LB.
- Physics: Coastline PHYS C185 + C280.
- Calculus: Coastline MATH C180 + C185 (Math 2A/2B), plus MATH C280 for 2D.

UC San Diego - Biology majors (2026-27 ASSIST):
- Chemistry: UCSD CHEM 6A, 6B, 6C and 7L are covered by Coastline CHEM C180/C180L + C185/C185L.
- Biology: UCSD BILD 1, BILD 2, BILD 3 and BILD 4 are covered by Coastline BIOL C180 + C185. BILD 5 has no Coastline course articulated.
- Math: UCSD calculus has two separate options on the agreement. Option A: MATH 10A is covered by Coastline MATH C140 (Business Calculus); MATH 10B has NO Coastline course articulated. Option B: MATH 20A and 20B are covered by Coastline MATH C180 + C185. Never say MATH C180 + C185 cover MATH 10A/10B — that mapping belongs to Option B (20A/20B) only. MATH 11 (probability and statistics) is covered by Coastline STAT C1000. The next course (10C/20C) has no Coastline equivalent on the agreement.
- Physics: UCSD PHYS 2A/2B/2C series is covered by Coastline PHYS C185 + C280 + C285 (2A <- PHYS C185; 2B <- PHYS C280; 2C <- the full three-course sequence). The PHYS 1A/1B/1C series has NO Coastline course articulated.

When comparing, present each school under its own cycle heading so the difference stays visible, and end with the different-cycles note above if you have not already stated it.
