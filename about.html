About This Knowledge Base

This website is the published knowledge base for Mr. Krabs, the S-STEM Scholar Assistant at Coastline College. The assistant helps S-STEM scholars and other Coastline students with transfer planning, course articulation questions, and program information.

Where the content comes from

Every note on this site is built from public sources, primarily the ASSIST articulation agreements between Coastline College and the receiving university for a stated agreement cycle. Notes quote the cycle printed on each agreement and never merge two different cycles into a single mapping. When a course has no articulated equivalent on the agreement, the notes say so explicitly.

How it is maintained

The notes are version-controlled and published here as ordinary web pages so the assistant can import the site as a knowledge source and stay in sync as notes are corrected or extended. Each page is a complete, standalone document: a summary page links to the individual notes, and every note carries its own navigation back to the rest of the site.
